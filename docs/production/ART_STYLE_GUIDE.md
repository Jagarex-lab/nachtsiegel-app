# ORAFOLD illustration direction

Working direction, 10 November 2026. Individual small characters, creatures and
objects are proposals under `docs/LORE-BIBLE.md`; the guide defines presentation,
not world history. Eira, the Archiv and the time correction retain their recorded
uncertainties. Do not promote the separate `docs/lore/` proposals to canon.

## Shared image language

Painterly material realism, clear silhouettes and a single readable focal action.
Use tactile weathered copper, cut glass, cloth, stone, living surfaces and measured
spatial folds. Prefer a specific local action over a distant interchangeable army
or generic armored figure. The upper-left source lights the focal subject; distant
atmosphere can soften while important faces, hands and anatomy remain sharp.

Generate only an illustration. All cost, title, type, species, rules, rarity,
statistics, power signs and frames are drawn by Godot. Exclude text, invented
lettering, borders, fake cards, UI, watermarks and known protected characters.
Frame and illustration must never be flattened into one delivery image.

The production master lifts the diagonal split, type/species and rules positions
by the same 60 master pixels for every card. This adds room for real three-ability
texts without reducing the 24-pixel master minimum. Names, costs, statistics,
rarity, outer silhouette and art source resolution remain invariant. The diagonal
mask leaves about 401 master pixels of average visible illustration height; no
individual card silently uses a smaller font or a different information layout.

The art window is 672:500 in the invariant 756 × 1056 master. Prefer native
1448 × 1086 or larger carefully reviewed sources, rather than enlarging an old
415-pixel atlas cell. Keep the main face, action and anatomy in the middle 80%; the
bottom right is partly concealed by the diagonal rules zone. Check that the crop
still communicates the subject at thumbnail size. Decorative rarity treatment
belongs to the deterministic frame and stays within the same outer contour.

Power identity is supported by material and composition as well as color. Rarity
must not override power identity. Do not imply that every rare creature is a ruler
or that a legendary card establishes a new canonical leader.

## Species review

The twelve technical species IDs stay unchanged. Humans need plausible hands and
faces; beasts coherent animal anatomy; spirits a legible independent presence;
constructs an intentional built structure; plants identifiable living growth;
amalgams coherent integrated origins; morphids visible self-change; paradoxes a
specific readable contradiction; revenants signs of an actually returned body;
elementals a controlled material phenomenon; devourers a clear consuming action;
mechanoids a visible predominant mechanical mechanism. Two species have equal
rank: both should be supported by the image when anatomy matters.

An echo is not a thirteenth species. Memory magic alone does not turn a human into
a spirit, and metal clothing alone does not turn a person into a mechanoid.
